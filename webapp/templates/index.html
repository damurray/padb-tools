3. Compare two datasets

      
Pairs two already-extracted CSVs (e.g. Santa Rosa vs. a second site) into one job that tags each row by site and reuses the normal pipeline — see boxplot's "Site Population Check" and the coverage-gap banner once it's run. Mainly useful when first standing up a new site — collapsed by default since it's a corner case otherwise.
